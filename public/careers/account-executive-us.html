About Alphaworx

Alphaworx builds the tools organizations use to run AI responsibly. AIOS Cloud is the platform for AI strategy and governance. It keeps strategy, initiatives, risk and governance in one place, with a record of who decided what and who owns the next step. It launches in the first quarter of 2027.

The CAIO Knowledge Base is the operating manual for the people accountable for AI. The AI Knowledge Base gives every team one shared path to learn AI and put it to work. Both are available now. Alphaworx Advisory works with leadership teams on AI strategy and governance, together with partner firms that sell and deliver our products in their markets.

Alphaworx is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and is growing its team ahead of the AIOS Cloud launch.

About the role

More and more leaders are being asked to show who approved each use of AI in their organization, what risks it carries and whether it's paying off. AIOS Cloud gives them one place to do that. The CAIO Knowledge Base and the AI Knowledge Base give their leaders and teams the knowledge to run it.

You'll find and close your own deals with enterprises, small businesses and government bodies, selling to the people accountable for AI: Chief AI Officers, CIOs, CTOs, heads of risk and compliance, and general counsel.

You'll own your pipeline from first contact to close, and work with leadership on positioning and on your largest deals.

What you'll do

  • Build your own pipeline through outbound, referrals, events and our published Insights.
  • Run discovery to learn how a buyer governs AI today, who owns the decisions and where it's breaking down.
  • Give demos of AIOS Cloud tailored to each buyer, from executives to the people who'll use it every day.
  • Take each deal from the first conversation to signature, including security and legal review, procurement and negotiation.
  • Sell team plans of the CAIO Knowledge Base and the AI Knowledge Base, on their own and alongside the platform.
  • Hand each new client to customer success with a clear record of what was agreed and why they bought.
  • Keep your pipeline and forecast accurate, so anyone can see where each deal stands.
  • Tell the product team what buyers ask for, which objections come up and where deals stall.

What you bring

  • Five or more years selling business software, with a record of closing deals with executive buyers and meeting or beating quota.
  • Pipeline you built yourself. You've sold without leads being handed to you.
  • Experience with long sales cycles involving several decision-makers, security review, legal and procurement.
  • The ability to learn a technical product well enough to demo it on your own and answer hard questions.
  • Clear, confident writing and speaking, with technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Comfort working remotely and managing your own territory.

Nice to have

  • Experience selling governance, risk and compliance, security, privacy or data software.
  • An existing network among Chief AI Officers, CIOs, or risk and compliance leaders.
  • Experience selling to government or to regulated industries such as financial services and healthcare.

Your first months

  • In your first month, you can demo AIOS Cloud and the knowledge bases on your own, and you've agreed a target account list with leadership.
  • By three months, you have a pipeline you built, and your first knowledge base deals are closing.
  • By six months, you're closing platform deals and have written down what works, so the team can build on it.

How we work

  • Remote. You work from wherever suits you, within the location given for the role.
  • We use AI tools in our own work every day, including agents that write code and draft documents. We expect you to use them too, and to check what they produce.
  • You own your area and the results that come with it, and you have the authority to act on what you find.
  • We write things down. Plans, decisions and reviews are kept in writing, so anyone can pick up where someone else left off.
