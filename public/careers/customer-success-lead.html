About Alphaworx

Alphaworx builds the tools organizations use to run AI responsibly. AIOS Cloud is the platform for AI strategy and governance. It keeps strategy, initiatives, risk and governance in one place, with a record of who decided what and who owns the next step. It launches in the first quarter of 2027.

The CAIO Knowledge Base is the operating manual for the people accountable for AI. The AI Knowledge Base gives every team one shared path to learn AI and put it to work. Both are available now. Alphaworx Advisory works with leadership teams on AI strategy and governance, together with partner firms that sell and deliver our products in their markets.

Alphaworx is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and is growing its team ahead of the AIOS Cloud launch.

About the role

AIOS Cloud launches in the first quarter of 2027. The first organizations to use it will judge it on how quickly it starts holding their real work: their AI strategy, their initiatives, their risks and the people who own them. You're the person who makes that happen.

You'll run each onboarding from the kickoff call to the first governance review held in the platform, then stay on as the client's main contact. You'll work with the leaders accountable for AI, often a Chief AI Officer, a CIO or a head of risk, and with the people on their teams who do the day-to-day work.

You'll help refine how we onboard and support clients, and what you hear from them feeds directly into what we build next.

What you'll do

  • Run onboarding for each new client, from kickoff to the first governance review held in AIOS Cloud.
  • Set up the platform to match the client's organization: its strategy, initiatives, risks, policies and owners.
  • Train client teams, from the executives who review the dashboards to the people who keep the records current.
  • Be the client's main contact after onboarding. Answer support questions and fix what you can the same day.
  • Hold regular reviews with each client's leadership on how they're using the platform and what's getting in the way.
  • Bring what clients ask for back to the product team, with enough detail that it can be built.
  • Write the onboarding guides, checklists and help articles that let the next client start faster.
  • Work with our partner firms when they bring clients to us, so those clients get the same start as everyone else.
  • Help the account executives in late-stage deals by explaining how onboarding works and what the first months look like.

What you bring

  • Five or more years onboarding, implementing or supporting enterprise software, in customer success, implementation or consulting.
  • A record of taking clients from signed contract to regular use, and keeping them.
  • Ease in front of senior leaders. You can run a meeting with a Chief AI Officer and leave with decisions made.
  • Clear writing. Busy executives read your onboarding plans and follow-up notes.
  • Working knowledge of AI governance, risk management or compliance, or a record of learning a new field quickly.
  • An eye for what slows clients down, and the drive to fix it.

Nice to have

  • Experience with governance, risk and compliance (GRC), security or privacy software.
  • Familiarity with the EU AI Act, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework or ISO/IEC 42001.
  • Experience working with clients in financial services, healthcare or government.

Your first months

  • In your first month, you know AIOS Cloud well enough to run a full onboarding on your own, and you've reviewed our onboarding plan and proposed improvements.
  • By three months, the first launch clients are set up and holding their governance reviews in the platform.
  • By six months, clients come to you first, the product team works from your feedback, and new clients get started faster than the first ones did.

How we work

  • Remote. You work from wherever suits you, within the location given for the role.
  • We use AI tools in our own work every day, including agents that write code and draft documents. We expect you to use them too, and to check what they produce.
  • You own your area and the results that come with it, and you have the authority to act on what you find.
  • We write things down. Plans, decisions and reviews are kept in writing, so anyone can pick up where someone else left off.

How to apply

Email jobs@alphaworx.io with the role in the subject line, a short note on why it fits you, and your résumé or LinkedIn profile. We discuss pay with each candidate.

How we handle applications is in our privacy notice.
