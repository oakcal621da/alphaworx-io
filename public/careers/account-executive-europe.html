About Alphaworx

Alphaworx builds the tools organizations use to run AI responsibly. AIOS Cloud is the platform for AI strategy and governance. It keeps strategy, initiatives, risk and governance in one place, with a record of who decided what and who owns the next step. It launches in the first quarter of 2027.

The CAIO Knowledge Base is the operating manual for the people accountable for AI. The AI Knowledge Base gives every team one shared path to learn AI and put it to work. Both are available now. Alphaworx Advisory works with leadership teams on AI strategy and governance, together with partner firms that sell and deliver our products in their markets.

Alphaworx is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and is growing its team ahead of the AIOS Cloud launch.

About the role

In Europe, the EU AI Act has made AI governance a legal duty for many organizations. They need to know which AI systems they use, how risky each one is and who's accountable for it. AIOS Cloud gives them one place to keep that record. The CAIO Knowledge Base and the AI Knowledge Base give their leaders and teams the knowledge to run it.

You'll find and close your own deals with enterprises, small businesses and public-sector bodies, selling to the people accountable for AI: Chief AI Officers, CIOs, data protection officers, and heads of risk and compliance.

This is a contract role. You'll own the European pipeline from first contact to close, and work with leadership on positioning and on your largest deals.

What you'll do

  • Build your own pipeline in Europe through outbound, referrals, events and our published Insights.
  • Run discovery to learn how a buyer governs AI today, what the EU AI Act and GDPR require of them, and who owns the decisions.
  • Give demos of AIOS Cloud tailored to each buyer, from executives to the people who'll use it every day.
  • Take each deal from the first conversation to signature, including security and legal review, procurement and negotiation.
  • Sell team plans of the CAIO Knowledge Base and the AI Knowledge Base, on their own and alongside the platform.
  • Hand each new client to customer success with a clear record of what was agreed and why they bought.
  • Keep your pipeline and forecast accurate, so anyone can see where each deal stands.
  • Tell the product team what European buyers ask for, including their language, data and regulatory needs.

What you bring

  • Five or more years selling business software in European markets, with a record of closing deals with executive buyers.
  • Pipeline you built yourself. You've sold without leads being handed to you.
  • Experience with long sales cycles involving several decision-makers, security review, legal and procurement.
  • Working knowledge of how the EU AI Act and GDPR shape what buyers need.
  • The ability to learn a technical product well enough to demo it on your own and answer hard questions.
  • Fluent business English, and experience working independently as a contractor or with a lot of autonomy.

Nice to have

  • Experience selling governance, risk and compliance, security, privacy or data software in Europe.
  • A network among Chief AI Officers, CIOs, data protection officers or risk leaders in one or more European countries.
  • Fluency in a second European business language.

Your first months

  • In your first month, you can demo AIOS Cloud and the knowledge bases on your own, and you've agreed target countries and accounts with leadership.
  • By three months, you have a European pipeline you built, and your first knowledge base deals are closing.
  • By six months, you're closing platform deals in Europe and have written down what works there.

How we work

  • Remote. You work from wherever suits you, within the location given for the role.
  • We use AI tools in our own work every day, including agents that write code and draft documents. We expect you to use them too, and to check what they produce.
  • You own your area and the results that come with it, and you have the authority to act on what you find.
  • We write things down. Plans, decisions and reviews are kept in writing, so anyone can pick up where someone else left off.
