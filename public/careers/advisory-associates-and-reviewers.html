About Alphaworx

Alphaworx builds the tools organizations use to run AI responsibly. AIOS Cloud is the platform for AI strategy and governance. It keeps strategy, initiatives, risk and governance in one place, with a record of who decided what and who owns the next step. It launches in the first quarter of 2027.

The CAIO Knowledge Base is the operating manual for the people accountable for AI. The AI Knowledge Base gives every team one shared path to learn AI and put it to work. Both are available now. Alphaworx Advisory works with leadership teams on AI strategy and governance, together with partner firms that sell and deliver our products in their markets.

Alphaworx is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and is growing its team ahead of the AIOS Cloud launch.

About the role

This is an open call for two kinds of contract work, for experienced practitioners in AI governance, risk, security, privacy and regulation.

Advisory associates work on client engagements with Alphaworx and our partner firms. Engagements cover AI strategy and assessment, governance and operating models, and executive advice. You'd take on a defined piece of an engagement, such as an assessment, a policy set or a governance design, and deliver it to the client with us.

Subject-matter reviewers check guides in the CAIO Knowledge Base and the AI Knowledge Base for accuracy in their field. You'd review a set of guides against current law, standards and practice, and tell us what's wrong, out of date or missing.

Work comes in defined pieces and fits around your other commitments. We agree the scope and the rate for each piece before it starts.

What you'll do

Advisory associates

  • Assess how a client uses AI today: what's in use, who owns it and the risks it carries.
  • Draft policies, standards and governance designs for a client's leadership to approve.
  • Lead or support workshops with executives and their teams.
  • Work with our partner firms on engagements in their markets.

Subject-matter reviewers

  • Review guides for accuracy against current law, standards and practice in your field.
  • Flag what's wrong, out of date or missing, and point to the source.
  • Suggest how a guide could be clearer for someone doing the job.

What you bring

  • At least eight years of practice in AI governance, risk, security, privacy or regulation, in industry, consulting, law or government.
  • For advisory associates, experience running client engagements and working with senior leaders.
  • For reviewers, depth in one jurisdiction, standard or industry.
  • Clear, precise writing, with claims you can source.
  • The judgment to say when something is outside your expertise.

Nice to have

  • Experience with the EU AI Act, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, or sector rules in financial services or healthcare.
  • Published work, teaching or standards work in your field.

How we work

  • Remote. You work from wherever suits you, within the location given for the role.
  • We use AI tools in our own work every day, including agents that write code and draft documents. We expect you to use them too, and to check what they produce.
  • You own your area and the results that come with it, and you have the authority to act on what you find.
  • We write things down. Plans, decisions and reviews are kept in writing, so anyone can pick up where someone else left off.
