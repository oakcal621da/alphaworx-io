About Alphaworx

Alphaworx builds the tools organizations use to run AI responsibly. AIOS Cloud is the platform for AI strategy and governance. It keeps strategy, initiatives, risk and governance in one place, with a record of who decided what and who owns the next step. It launches in the first quarter of 2027.

The CAIO Knowledge Base is the operating manual for the people accountable for AI. The AI Knowledge Base gives every team one shared path to learn AI and put it to work. Both are available now. Alphaworx Advisory works with leadership teams on AI strategy and governance, together with partner firms that sell and deliver our products in their markets.

Alphaworx is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, and is growing its team ahead of the AIOS Cloud launch.

About the role

AIOS Cloud holds an organization's AI strategy, initiatives, risks, policies and decisions, along with the record of who approved what. Clients trust it with sensitive information and will rely on it in front of auditors and regulators. It has to be correct, secure and available when they need it.

You'll ship features across the whole platform and own how it runs in production: deployments, monitoring, security and incident response.

We build with AI coding agents every day, and much of our code is written and reviewed with their help. The engineer's job is to direct that work, check it carefully and own the result. You'll set the standards those agents work to.

What you'll do

  • Design, build and ship features across AIOS Cloud, from the database to the screens clients use.
  • Work on a platform built with Python and PostgreSQL and deployed in the cloud.
  • Own reliability: deployments, monitoring, backups and recovery, and the response when something breaks.
  • Own security: sign-in, access controls, keeping each client's data separate, and audit records that hold up to review.
  • Direct and review the work of AI coding agents, and set the tests and checks their work has to pass.
  • Write tests and keep the test suite fast and trustworthy.
  • Work with customer success on client issues, and with product leadership on what to build next.
  • Help clients' security teams through their reviews of AIOS Cloud.

What you bring

  • Seven or more years building and running production web applications, several of them in a senior role.
  • Strong Python and SQL, and experience running PostgreSQL in production.
  • A security-minded approach to multi-tenant data, authentication and audit trails.
  • Experience owning systems in production: deployments, monitoring and incident response.
  • Practice working with AI coding tools, and the judgment to check what they produce before it ships.
  • Clear writing. You can explain a technical decision to a client's security team or to someone outside engineering.

Nice to have

  • Experience with SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 or a similar security program.
  • Experience building governance, risk, compliance or workflow software.
  • Experience with Flask.

Your first months

  • In your first month, you've shipped to production and know how the platform is built, deployed and monitored.
  • By three months, you own deployments and incident response, and you've closed the gaps you found in testing and monitoring.
  • By six months, AIOS Cloud is running for launch clients, and you've set the engineering standards the team works to.

How we work

  • Remote. You work from wherever suits you, within the location given for the role.
  • We use AI tools in our own work every day, including agents that write code and draft documents. We expect you to use them too, and to check what they produce.
  • You own your area and the results that come with it, and you have the authority to act on what you find.
  • We write things down. Plans, decisions and reviews are kept in writing, so anyone can pick up where someone else left off.

How to apply

Email jobs@alphaworx.io with the role in the subject line, a short note on why it fits you, and your résumé or LinkedIn profile. We discuss pay with each candidate.

How we handle applications is in our privacy notice.
