01 / Enterprise

Bring AI activity into one operating picture.

For leadership teams coordinating AI across business units, functions, and suppliers.

  • Align the portfolio. Connect proposed initiatives to strategic priorities, accountable owners, benefit cases, and investment decisions.
  • Make governance consistent. Establish common policy and review requirements, with responsibilities and evidence recorded for each initiative.
  • Maintain leadership oversight. Bring open commitments, risk, value, and decisions into a repeatable management review.
Discuss your organization
02 / Small business

Practical structure. Room to grow.

For owners and small teams introducing AI while keeping day-to-day work moving.

  • Know what is in use. Record AI tools and workflows, who uses them, and who takes responsibility for each one.
  • Set clear working rules. Establish practical guidance for information handling, acceptable use, review, and escalation.
  • Choose the next useful step. Capture opportunities, agree ownership, and review whether the work is delivering the intended benefit.
Discuss your organization
03 / Government entities

Advance AI with clear accountability.

For public-sector leaders connecting AI opportunities to service outcomes, institutional authority, and ongoing oversight.

  • Establish authority and scope. Record sponsors, accountable owners, decision rights, and the organizational boundaries for AI use.
  • Support evidence-based review. Connect proposals to data readiness, supplier considerations, risk reviews, and supporting evidence.
  • Retain the decision trail. Maintain linked decisions, reviews, assigned actions, and ongoing oversight records.
