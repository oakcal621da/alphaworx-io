Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated November 25, 2026

This notice explains how Alphaworx Intelligence LLC handles information when you visit this website, send an inquiry, schedule a conversation, or apply for a role. Contact us at info@alphaworx.io about privacy or your information.

Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, work email, conversation topic, and message. Company and role are optional. If you choose to schedule a conversation, the Alphaworx scheduler asks for the information needed to create the appointment, including your name, email address, selected time, and related appointment details. We use these details to respond, discuss potential services, arrange the requested meeting, and keep a record of our conversation. Providing them is voluntary; without the required contact or booking information, we cannot respond or schedule the meeting.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information, confidential client material, passwords, or other information that is unnecessary for an initial conversation.

Retention

For inquiries and appointments that do not become client engagements, we delete the related correspondence and calendar records within 12 months after the last contact. Client records are handled according to the engagement and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Information needed to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a dispute may be kept longer for that purpose.

The contact service keeps temporary submission identifiers, keyed digests, timestamps, and delivery states to limit abuse and avoid duplicates. Entries older than 24 hours are removed when the service next processes a valid submission. This record does not contain message text. Provider logs and backups follow the relevant provider's retention processes.

Job applications

If you apply through a role page or write to jobs@alphaworx.io, we use what you send (your name and contact details, a link to your résumé or LinkedIn profile, and your message) to consider you for that role or similar work, and to reply to you. We delete applications within 12 months.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your inquiry information by emailing info@alphaworx.io. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict processing, obtain a portable copy, or complain to a data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us to explain your request. We will consider it under the law that applies to your information.

Updates

We will update this notice when the site's data practices change and show the revised date at the top of this page.
