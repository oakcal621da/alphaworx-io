Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated November 23, 2026

This notice explains how Alphaworx Intelligence LLC handles information when you visit this website, send an inquiry, or schedule a conversation. Contact us at info@alphaworx.io about privacy or your information.

Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, work email, conversation topic, and message. Company and role are optional. If you choose to schedule a conversation, the Alphaworx scheduler asks for the information needed to create the appointment, including your name, email address, selected time, and related appointment details. We use these details to respond, discuss potential services, arrange the requested meeting, and keep a record of our conversation. Providing them is voluntary; without the required contact or booking information, we cannot respond or schedule the meeting.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information, confidential client material, passwords, or other information that is unnecessary for an initial conversation.

How the website and form work

Render hosts the website and the service that handles contact forms and scheduling requests. Cloudflare provides web delivery and protection in the hosting path. Form submissions are delivered to our Google Workspace inbox. The scheduler checks availability and creates calendar invitations and Google Meet details through Google Calendar. These providers process the information needed to provide their services. Hosting and security systems may record technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser details, and request times.

The interactive assessment uses fictional examples; it does not scan your company's systems. Form drafts stay in the current page until you send them or leave the page. If direct sending is unavailable and you choose “Continue in email,” your email app opens a draft for you to send.

Our purposes and use

We use inquiry information for the conversation you requested and to operate and protect this website. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to a newsletter or an automated marketing campaign. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate visits, page views, referral sources, and page performance. Cloudflare states that this service does not use cookies or track individual visitors. The website does not use advertising pixels, and its own code does not set tracking cookies. Infrastructure providers may use technologies needed for delivery and security.

Where UK or European data protection law applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to relevant business inquiries and securing our website, and on steps requested before entering a contract where applicable. We do not use this form to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects through automated processing.

Retention

For inquiries and appointments that do not become client engagements, we delete the related correspondence and calendar records within 12 months after the last contact. Client records are handled according to the engagement and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Information needed to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a dispute may be kept longer for that purpose.

The contact service keeps temporary submission identifiers, keyed digests, timestamps, and delivery states to limit abuse and avoid duplicates. Entries older than 24 hours are removed when the service next processes a valid submission. This record does not contain message text. Provider logs and backups follow the relevant provider's retention processes.

Service providers and international processing

Alphaworx is based in Dallas, Texas. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Their privacy information explains their own handling and transfer arrangements: Render, Cloudflare, and Google (Workspace mail, Calendar and Meet). Contact us for information about arrangements relevant to your inquiry.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your inquiry information by emailing info@alphaworx.io. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict processing, obtain a portable copy, or complain to a data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us to explain your request. We will consider it under the law that applies to your information.

Updates

We will update this notice when the site's data practices change and show the revised date at the top of this page.
