02 / Map actual AI use

Four process families. Four distinct AI assessments.

MANUFACTURING / $14M COST

Vision models inspect products.

Check defect detection, missed defects, false rejects, scrap, rework, overrides, and drift after a line or product change.

MAINTENANCE / $12M COST

Models predict equipment failures.

Check alert precision, useful warning time, technician action, and whether “avoided downtime” has a defensible comparison.

ORDER OPERATIONS / $10M COST

AI reads, drafts, and invokes tools.

Trace order extraction, customer responses, and credit recommendations through correction, approval, and final posting.

ENGINEERING / $16M COST

Copilots generate code and retrieve designs.

Inspect actual adoption, review effort, defects, IP boundaries, duplicated licenses, and whether released time changes delivery.

Four families total $52m, including allocated shared costs.Evaluate distinct models, users, data, actions, and business outcomes. Shared spending is counted once.

Meridian Industrial Group is fictional. Evidence, figures, and decisions are illustrative.

10 / Retest before expansion

Scale what survives the right test.

VISION / QUALITY GATE

Test at the production line.

Measure escaped defects and false rejects against a matched production baseline. Retest when the product, camera, lighting, or line changes.

MAINTENANCE / VALUE GATE

Connect alerts to work orders.

Trace precision, lead time, intervention, and outcome. Adjust comparisons for asset condition and scheduled maintenance before crediting avoided downtime.

GENERATIVE AI / CONTROL GATE

Inspect the completed work.

Evaluate corrected orders, reviewed code, and resolved enquiries. Test data boundaries, unsafe tool calls, approval bypasses, and fallback procedures.

PORTFOLIO / FUNDING GATE

Accept the incremental case.

Finance and process owners sign the evidence, coverage, costs, and benefit mechanism. New automation authority gets a separate release decision.

Define owners, thresholds, samples, and stop rules per use. Broad AI adoption, a successful demo, or one high-performing site does not establish enterprise readiness.

Illustrative assessment and acceptance tests; an evaluation pass supports a bounded decision, not a guarantee of future behavior.
