The leadership test

Five questions.
A clearer strategy.

These are the conversations that turn an AI initiative into something an enterprise can own and operate.

Explore the Meridian assessment walkthrough ↗
01What business outcome must change?＋

Name the workflow, its current baseline, the target outcome, and the person who owns the value. Consider whether AI is the right intervention.

Bring to the table: a measurable business problem.

02Who can make the hard decisions?＋

Make funding, approval, escalation, and stop decisions explicit. A sponsor needs a mandate that survives competing priorities.

Bring to the table: named decision-makers and decision rights.

03What evidence earns the next investment?＋

Agree what a pilot must demonstrate before it scales. Include quality, workflow adoption, full operating cost, and business value.

Bring to the table: a baseline, evaluation plan, and hold or stop criteria.

04Where are the boundaries?＋

Define which data, actions, and commitments the system may handle. Establish human review, escalation, and recovery appropriate to the use case.

Bring to the table: approved scope, permissions, and an accountable risk owner.

05How will the strategy stay current?＋

Set a recurring review of outcomes, costs, adoption, incidents, and vendor changes. Use the evidence to renew priorities and retire work that no longer earns its place.

Bring to the table: a review calendar and a decision record.
