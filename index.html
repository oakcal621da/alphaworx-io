How we build

Every system has to survive its own argument.

Most AI systems are built to sound right. Ours are built to be checked. Every output — a research brief, a trade recommendation, a forecast — runs through a structured adversarial review before a human ever sees it. It has to survive being argued with by itself first.

Adversarial by design

Every draft is reviewed by the system itself before it's reviewed by anyone else — assume it's wrong until it proves otherwise.

Test-driven, always

Engineering standard, not an afterthought. No dead code, no untested paths shipped into production.

Validated out-of-sample

Forecasts and models are checked against data they've never seen — not tuned to look right in hindsight.

What we've built
AIR
Atlas Intelligence Research

An autonomous, tool-equipped research agent. Given a topic, it researches primary sources, drafts a brief, and then runs that draft through a five-layer adversarial self-review — arguing with its own conclusions — before revising and publishing. Every claim in a finished brief traces back to a primary source.

See it live — The Primer Desk
ATRE
Atlas Trading & Risk Engine

A quantitative portfolio and risk management system. It models market uncertainty and prices the gap between what a forecast implies and what the market is already pricing in — turning that gap into position-sizing and trade decisions, gated by the same validation discipline behind every system we build.

Internal system — not publicly deployed.

About

Built around one discipline.

Alphaworx is a Dallas-based software company built around a single idea: an AI system isn't finished when it works — it's finished when it's been tried to break, and it survived. Founded and led by Don Addison, whose 20+ years diagnosing what's actually broken — across trading floors, an aviation turnaround, and energy investing — became the instinct behind how every Alphaworx system gets built.
